About Civic Telemetry™

An independent election-evidence project published by OrbitWorks Labs LLC (d/b/a OrbitWorks). It reports what official election sources published, and when. It never calls a race.

How the record is kept

Automated collectors pull machine-readable returns directly from state election offices (the Texas Secretary of State and the published feeds of other states listed in the source notices) and from one independent aggregator. Every raw response is archived by content hash. Official counts are cross-checked against the aggregator, and any disagreement is flagged on the page rather than resolved silently. Each published edition is hash-chained into an append-only ledger whose integrity is verified automatically on every change; the full record, including the verification tool, is public at github.com/calenb2/civic-telemetry-election-watch.
