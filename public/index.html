UNDERSTANDING THIS EDITION

Dated election evidence · Read the reporting cutoffs

Civic Telemetry is an independent analysis project, not an election authority. Participation figures are not candidate vote results.

How to read this dashboard

Participation is not preference. Turnout shows reported participation, not candidate choices. Primary-voting history does not establish how someone voted.

Different reporting cutoffs. Counties and publishers update at different times. Each figure retains its source and observation date; a shared date label does not establish an identical cutoff.

Analysis is identified separately. Interpretations and polling signals are not official election results or race calls.

Incomplete evidence stays visible. Missing, inaccessible and unverified information remains marked. Missing data is not zero.

Preliminary means preliminary. Reported returns, race calls and officially canvassed results are distinct. Counts may be revised as processing continues.

Three separate clocks. Source cutoff, research review and successful publication describe different events. A new audit or page refresh does not make the election data newer.

See methodology, sources and publication status. For voting instructions, use VoteTexas.gov or your county election office.

Calendar basis: Texas Secretary of State, November 3 election calendar. Guidance updated November 1, 2026; this is not a data-review timestamp.
